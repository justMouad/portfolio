About me

I'm a Data Engineering and Data Science student in Fès. After a Bachelor's in Mathematics and Computer Science, I'm now completing a Master's in Data Engineering and Data Science at FSDM, alongside a double-degree Master's in Data Science with Université Sorbonne Paris Nord.

I enjoy designing data platforms that keep analytical tables fresh and trustworthy: capturing changes from operational databases, streaming them through Kafka, transforming them with Spark and dbt, and checking quality between layers. My internship at CIH Bank taught me how to build pipelines under the constraints of sensitive financial data.

I also work with machine learning tools such as scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow and Keras, and I'm looking to grow in scalable data solutions, data science and AI.
